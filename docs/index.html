Bunny Receipt
daily task ledger
================================
A tiny desktop companion that keeps a receipt of your day. Add tasks, check them off, and let your bunny cheer you on.
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
What's on the receipt
  • [x] Daily task list with receipt-style UI
  • [x] Animated bunny mascot that reacts to your progress
  • [x] Bunny walks onto your desktop to check on you
  • [x] Carry over yesterday's unfinished tasks
  • [x] System tray, always-on-top, and morning reminders
  • [x] Four paper tints, sound effects, and confetti
  • [x] 7-day history of your daily receipts
  • [x] Print animation with sound on every launch
